A lender uploads a model and a feature dictionary. An agent asks, in language, what it would cost to fake each field versus truly change it. Those judgements become a mutability table. The engine, not the language model, produces the numbers.

Think of a locksmith. They do not only ask whether the lock opens for the key. They ask whether a cheap copy works, whether the bolt is attached to anything, whether a whole neighbourhood of doors fail the same way. Janus is that inspection for a scorecard.

Who uses it? Model risk, credit risk, and anyone who has to explain a decline to a regulator or to themselves.
