FindHackathons collects hackathons and data-science competitions, enriches them, and puts a shortlist in front of you fast.

### Filter, not a school
It ranks finishable events. It does not promise you will win.

### Enriched listings
Scraped events are cleaned and scored so a beginner is not sent into an impossible contest.

### Alerts on a cadence
Onboarding and alerts sit on top of a ranked feed, not a raw dump of every listing on the web.
