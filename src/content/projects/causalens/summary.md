CausaLens turns regional news into deduplicated events and evidence-backed causal edges, then lets you walk the graph.

### Events, not articles
Many outlets cover the same expansion. Those write-ups merge into one event before any edge is drawn.

### Evidence on the edge
Supporting articles sit on the relationship. Observed edges are solid, inferred dashed, predicted dotted and labelled as not established fact.

### Three walks
WHY? walks upstream. WHAT NEXT? walks downstream. REGIONAL RIPPLE highlights cross-border effects.
