Most news products are good at one job: telling you what happened. A factory expands. A fundraise lands. A power constraint shows up in Singapore. Each story is a fragment.

If you trade, operate, or make policy in Southeast Asia, the fragment is not enough. Capital, factories, power, and policy couple Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia, and Thailand. You need the chain: why it happened, what sits downstream, and which neighbouring markets might feel it.
