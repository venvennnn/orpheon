Business software still skips the layer engineering takes for granted. Code has development, staging, and production. A pricing change, a policy, or an assistant prompt often has only idea and production.

The first people who feel that idea are real customers. Aftermath inserts a rehearsal in between: a seeded world of synthetic customers who live through the proposed change so the cascade can be inspected before it ships.
