Aftermath is a staging environment for business decisions. It builds a fake world of customers, lets them live through a proposed change, and reports what happened — including chain reactions a slide deck never shows.

### Rehearsal, not a dashboard
The interesting object is a month of lives and a named failure pattern, not a single KPI after the fact.

### Customers with memory
Synthetic people have a plan, a temper, a churn threshold, and a history. They are not survey rows.

### Counterfactuals beside the proposal
A milder universe sits next to the one you proposed, so a decision is not compared only to a void.
