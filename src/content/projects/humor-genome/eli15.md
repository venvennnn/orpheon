Most comedy tools write jokes. This one tries to explain why a joke works, or why it does not, and how it might land with different crowds.

You paste text, or you upload a short clip. For text, Gemma is documented as decomposing setup, expectation, violation, payoff; listing mechanisms; finding punchlines; scoring a six-axis “humor genome” (surprise, specificity, cleverness, relatability, edge, warmth); and suggesting punch-ups.

For video, the README describes detecting where the crowd actually laughs, sampling frames for multimodal Gemma, and overlaying predicted laughs from the transcript against measured laughter. The gaps are the story.

Who is this for? People studying humor, not a punchline vending machine.
