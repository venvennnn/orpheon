Protected forests now have cheap recorders. What they do not have is a way to turn months of audio into a patrol route tonight. The hard drive comes back to a lab months later. By the time someone confirms illegal extraction on the tape, the trees are already gone.

Forest Sentinel is framed as an edge listening post, not a toy classifier. It is supposed to reason locally about what is happening in a protected area and send only what matters over a thin link — LoRa, satellite, or SMS — because fourteen days of raw audio will not fit.

Who is this for? Rangers and conservation teams who have more audio than bandwidth. What goes in is forest sound. What comes out is a decision about where to walk, not a 19 GB file.
