Forest Sentinel is framed as an edge listening post, documented as powered by Gemma: reason locally, transmit only what matters.

### Bandwidth is the constraint
Fourteen days of raw audio will not fit on LoRa, satellite, or SMS.

### A decision, not a file
What comes out is where to walk tonight, not a 19 GB archive.

### Local demo
A Streamlit app with cached backend and generated demo data, no login required.
